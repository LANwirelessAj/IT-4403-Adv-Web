What I learned testing the URLs (Parts 1 and 3)

ParameterWhat it doesExample
qThe search term. Changing data%20analysis to web%20development changed every book on this page.q=data%20analysis
limitHow many results come back. Changing it from 10 to 5 cut the list to 5 books.limit=10
pageWhich page of results to return. Pages start at 1, so page 2 with a limit of 10 is results 11 to 20.page=2
offsetHow many results to skip. An offset of 30 with a limit of 30 returns results 31 to 60.offset=30

A search response wraps its books in the docs array, and each one has a key like /works/OL45804W. Adding .json to that key gives the work record URL, https://openlibrary.org/works/OL45804W.json, which describes one book instead of a list.
