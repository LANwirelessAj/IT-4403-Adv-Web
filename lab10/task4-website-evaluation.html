Task 4: Mobile Website Evaluation

A checklist for reviewing weather.gov and cobb.com on a phone, with a place for your own notes.

How to Evaluate

Open each site on a phone, or in Chrome DevTools device mode (Ctrl + Shift + M) with a phone like the iPhone 12 or Pixel 7 selected. Try it in portrait and landscape, then jot down what works and what doesn't under each heading. Your notes save in this browser as you type.

National Weather Service

Open www.weather.gov (opens in a new tab)

Layout

Does the page fit the screen, or do you have to scroll sideways or pinch to zoom? Do columns stack in a sensible order?

Navigation

Is the menu easy to open and tap? Are buttons and links big enough and far enough apart for a finger? Can you find the main tasks quickly?

Readability

Is the text big enough without zooming? Is there enough contrast? Do images, maps, and tables resize or get cut off?
