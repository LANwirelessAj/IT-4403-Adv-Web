About This Site

This site holds my lab work and individual project milestones for IT 4403. The project builds step by step toward a single page application that reads and displays book data from Open Library.

I'm a senior at Kennesaw State University, currently serving as an ITAM Coordinator at Minebea AccessSolutions and as an officer in the Georgia Army National Guard. I'm ambitious, always ready to learn, and building toward a career at the intersection of IT and leadership.
