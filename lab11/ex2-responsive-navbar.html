Exercise 2: Responsive Navigation Menu

Breakpoint: xs (<576px)sm (576px+)md (768px+)lg (992px+)xl (1200px+)
Continuing build: Exercise 1b's column drop layout plus a responsive navbar.

About the Navbar

navbar-expand-lg keeps the five links in a row from 992px up. Below that they collapse behind the hamburger button, which uses Bootstrap's collapse plugin (from bootstrap.bundle.min.js) to open and close the menu. sticky-top keeps the bar visible while scrolling, and the button's aria-expanded and aria-controls tell screen readers what it opens.

Column Drop Layout

Three columns sit side by side on large screens. At the md breakpoint the third column drops below the first two, and on phones all three stack.
